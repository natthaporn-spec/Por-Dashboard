# Por-Dashboard

### Modern Sales & Inventory Management System

</div>

---

## 📖 About the Project

Por-Dashboard is a modern sales and inventory management system designed to help retail stores and businesses manage their daily operations more efficiently. The system was developed to simplify product management, sales processing, stock control, customer management, and business reporting within a single platform. It helps reduce operational complexity, minimize human errors, and improve the overall efficiency of store management.

The system allows business owners to manage products, monitor inventory movement, process sales transactions, and organize customer information accurately and systematically. In addition, the platform can generate useful reports such as sales summaries, profit analysis, and inventory reports to support better business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Manage products and inventory accurately  
- Simplify and speed up sales operations  
- Reduce errors caused by manual calculations and paperwork  
- Monitor stock movement and low-stock products  
- Organize customer information and sales history efficiently  
- Generate sales, profit, and inventory reports  
 
---

## 🚀 Benefits of the System

- Improves customer service speed and efficiency  
- Reduces time spent managing inventory  
- Helps businesses plan product purchasing more effectively  
- Keeps store data organized and easy to access
- Supports business growth and future scalability
  
---

## 👨‍💼 Target Users

This system is suitable for:

- Retail stores  
- Online shops  
- Convenience stores  
- Small and Medium-sized Businesses (SMEs)  
- Businesses that require inventory and sales management systems
